Privacy Policy

PixelFix Pro is a static browser-based utility. Image processing runs in your browser and this project does not include a server upload endpoint. PDF tools load PDF.js and jsPDF from a third-party CDN, so your browser connects to that provider to download the libraries. The site owner/hosting provider may collect standard server logs. Do not use this site for highly sensitive documents unless you understand your browser, network and hosting environment.

This starter policy is not legal advice. Review and adapt it to your actual hosting, analytics, ads, cookies and jurisdiction before publishing.
